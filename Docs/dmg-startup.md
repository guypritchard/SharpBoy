# Optional DMG startup

`new Gameboy(enableDmgStartup: true)` selects a **synthetic, clean-room** animation; bare `new Gameboy()` skips it (including compiler ROM validation). Console and debugger opt in. No Nintendo boot ROM code or binary is executed, stored, or bundled. Every call to `Step` or `RunStep` advances exactly one 70,224-cycle LCD frame, clocks timer/video/serial/audio, and leaves PC at `$0100` until the handoff. `ExecuteLoadedRom` completes the same startup before executing cartridge instructions. The console paces those cycles against the wall clock; the debugger can trace or step back one frame at a time.

The sequence clears VRAM, sets `NR52=80`, `NR11=80`, `NR12=F3`, `NR51=F3`, `NR50=77`, `BGP=FC`, doubles both nibbles of each of the **cartridge's** 48 logo bytes into one-bitplane rows, and packs them contiguously into 24 tiles (`$8010–$818F`, tiles 1–24). The registered-symbol tile is tile 25 at `$8190–$819F`. Two map rows (`$9904–$990F` and `$9924–$992F`) display all 24 logo tiles, with the symbol at `$9910`. `LCDC=91`, `SCY=64h` begin the scroll. SCY is 100 in emitted frame 1, drops to 99 in emitted frame 2, reaches zero in emitted frame 200, then stays zero for 64 more frames: **264 frames = 18,539,136 cycles = ~4.42 seconds** at 4,194,304 Hz. Each even frame's scroll/tone update precedes that frame's hardware clock and `FrameReady` event. At scroll counts `$62` and `$64` (frames 196 and 200), channel 1 is triggered with `NR13=83/NR14=87` and `NR13=C1/NR14=87`. The period values `$783` and `$7C1` produce `131072/(2048−period)` = ~1048.576 Hz and ~2080.508 Hz; the `F3` envelope decays each tone.

Unlike actual DMG hardware, a missing, short, or mismatched logo **only issues `Trace.TraceWarning`** (including title and mismatch/length) and never locks the system. The supplied bytes are displayed as-is; missing bytes become blank. The header checksum is not a gate. At handoff PC is `$0100`, AF=`01B0`, BC=`0013`, DE=`00D8`, HL=`014D`, SP=`FFFE`, SCY/SCX=`00`, LCDC=`91`, BGP=`FC`, IE=`00`, and IF has VBlank pending. Snapshot/restore includes frame progress alongside hardware state. Normal `CartridgeLoader` header validation is unchanged.
